Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShortsSync ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access or use our applications and services.

Key Principle: We value your privacy. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or video content. All OAuth tokens and credentials are encrypted and stored locally on your machine.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our application and connect your TikTok account, we may process:

  • TikTok Profile Information: We request basic public profile information via official TikTok OAuth (such as your display name, profile avatar, and account identifier) solely to confirm authentication and display your active profile in the app interface.
  • Authentication Tokens: Temporary OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens generated by TikTok to authorize video uploads on your behalf.
  • Video & Media Content: Video files, descriptions, and media tags that you specifically choose to upload or schedule for publishing to your TikTok profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data strictly for the following operational purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and facilitate connection with the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • To transmit and publish your selected video content directly to your TikTok account.
  • To maintain system functionality and troubleshoot operational errors.

3. TikTok API Scopes & Data Usage

Our application interacts with the TikTok Developer Platform using the following scopes:

  • user.info.basic: Read basic profile information to display your connected channel state.
  • video.upload & video.publish: Upload and publish videos that you explicitly command the app to submit.

We do not access or read your private messages, contacts, payment methods, or other private platform data.

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Encryption: OAuth tokens and secrets are stored locally using system-level secure credential storage (such as OS Keychain / Credential Manager DPAPI).
  • No Cloud Harvesting: We do not transfer your personal TikTok login credentials or user tokens to any external tracking or advertising servers.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Data is communicated only to official TikTok endpoints (https://open.tiktokapis.com) necessary to perform publishing actions that you initiate.

6. Data Retention & Revocation

  • Disconnecting Account: You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time directly in the app settings, which deletes all locally cached tokens.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke access at any time via your TikTok account settings: Settings > Security > Manage app permissions.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at: privacy@shortssync.app.
